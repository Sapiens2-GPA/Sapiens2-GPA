Sapiens2-GPA:
Aligning Latent Visual and Geometric Representations for Robust 3D Head Expression Estimation

Wen Hao He, Jiacheng Wu, Jie Chen†
Hong Kong Baptist University

Abstract

Parametric 3D head estimation from a single image requires jointly reasoning about detailed geometry, facial expression, head pose, and camera configuration. We propose a two-stage framework that aligns visual features with a unified geometric prior. First, HeadVAE learns a structured latent representation from canonical, ordered FLAME point clouds, jointly capturing fine-grained geometry and interpretable FLAME parameters through geometric reconstruction, parameter supervision, decoded-surface supervision, and dual-branch consistency. Second, we introduce the Sapiens2 Geometry and Placement Adapter (Sapiens2-GPA), which reads frozen contextual tokens from a pre-trained Sapiens2 encoder while preserving its original semantic stream. Experiments show high-quality geometric reconstruction and FLAME parameter estimation over feed-forward baselines, with qualitatively robust performance under visual domain shift.
